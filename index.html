Research Definition

For each target draw, the engine builds four historical pools: previous 4 draws, previous 5 draws, previous 6 draws, and previous 7 draws.

The four pools are combined into one 4567 Master Pool. The target draw is then compared against that pool.

This engine is a research/backtesting tool. It does not establish predictive odds or guarantee future lottery results.
